Welcome to the kitchen

Everything here is tiny grains with a temperature. Recipes are real ratios: mix the right amounts and the bowl tells you what you made, or what's missing.

  1. Press Run in the program under the kitchen. Four dispensers weigh out flour, sugar, egg and butter, the mixer makes cake batter, and it drains into the tin.
  2. The belt carries the tin to the oven. The oven is on Auto bake: it opens, pulls the tin in, shuts, bakes 20 s and sends the cake out the other side onto a cooling belt.
  3. Bake until golden. Heat view shows the heat reaching the middle.

Click any machine with the Hand to change it: ingredient, grams, color, speed.
